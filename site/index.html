AppSmoothly

You, your customer, and an agent — in one room, around the clock.

AppSmoothly gives an app its own room: you and your customer talk in it, and an agent that never leaves turns the talk into working software. It refines the idea with you, builds it, reviews its own work, and hands you a version to click through. Say yes and it ships. Set up loops — a daily report, a cleanup, a check — and they keep running while you sleep.

The agent lives inside the app, not beside it. It can look something up in your data when the customer asks, prepare an invoice, send a mail or an SMS, fix the bug you just described in plain words. Between all that, it costs nothing but the small server it lives on.

For developers

Under the hood it is boring on purpose. Plain Ruby on Rails; the workshop — agent, previews, loops — runs on one VPS, and production runs on its own. One container per piece of work, the whole small-app factory included: every change is a branch, a preview forked from production data, and a pull request — nothing crosses to the production server unreviewed. The base is opinionated and agent-ready, with guardrails, secrets that never touch the chat, and a build template that tracks main by itself. That is what makes it safe to ship a small niche app for a customer and then invite that customer into the room. When AI is not enough, any Rails freelancer picks up a normal codebase. Bring your own Claude subscription; idle cost is the VPS and backups.
