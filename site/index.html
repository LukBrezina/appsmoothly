A chat where your features get built.

Every project gets its own development cell: a private chat with an agent that codes, and disposable sandboxes where each feature runs on its own live URL — branch, preview, pull request included.

How it works

  1. Paste a repo URL into your project’s chat. The agent sets everything up and asks for secrets through one-time links — never through the chat itself.
  2. Ask for features in plain words — from your laptop or your phone. Each one gets its own room, its own sandbox, its own preview link.
  3. Click the preview, merge the PR. The sandbox disappears; the next one boots from your latest main in seconds.

Why it holds up

  • One project, one VM. Hard isolation, snapshotted daily, disposable by design.
  • Previews that sleep. Idle sandboxes freeze to nothing and wake the moment you visit.
  • Always current. The build template tracks your main branch on its own.
  • Secrets stay secrets. Tokens and keys travel by one-time link, never in a transcript.
  • Boring parts, on purpose. Git, systemd, containers, Campfire — everything inspectable, ssh always one hop away.

zzz… a sleeping preview costs nothing. Visiting it is the alarm clock.
