AppSmoothly

Your own app, on your own server, with an agent inside.

AppSmoothly is a setup you launch on a cheap VPS. Pick a base app (self-hosted tools like Campfire or Fizzy work today), connect the repo and your Claude subscription, and you get a chat. The agent in it lives next to your app: it changes code, queries your data, prepares things for customers. Your customers just use the app.

This is not vibe coding. When you ask for a feature, the agent builds it in a sandbox forked from the latest production data, on its own preview URL, behind a pull request. Production changes only after you have clicked through the preview. The stack is plain Ruby on Rails, so if the agent gets stuck, any Rails freelancer can take over. Updates apply themselves. When you are not building, the bill is the VPS and the backups.
